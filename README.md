# Lumen downloads

Lumen is one workspace for everything you write, plan and track: a block editor and a database in one,
synced to every device in real time. Your pages live in the cloud, on our servers in Amsterdam, inside the EU.

Use it in the browser at **[lumen.jeanluc.online](https://lumen.jeanluc.online)**, or download the apps here.

## Mac

**[Download Lumen for Mac](https://github.com/jeanluc033/lumen-releases/releases/latest/download/Lumen-mac-universal.dmg)**
(universal: Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 12 or later)

Open the disk image and drag Lumen to Applications. The app is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by
Apple, so it opens without warnings. Sign in with your Lumen account.
